Eigengrau

angle basin, debut album, 6 november 2026, five tracks, 42:30

album

Eigengrau is the debut album from Angle Basin, the ambient techno project of Aaron Steven White. The album was written, produced and mixed by White in Rochester, NY, and mastered by Giuseppe Tillieci (Neel) at Enisslab in Rome. It is five long-form tracks, moving between beatless stretches and long, heavy grooves.

Reference points: Monolake, Voices from the Lake, Gas.

Release date: 6 Nov 2026
